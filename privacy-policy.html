Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect and how we use it

NXT Fitout Technical Service may receive information you choose to provide when contacting us via WhatsApp, Instagram or other direct channels: your name, phone number, profile/contact details, messages, project location, photographs, measurements and requirements. We use this information to respond to enquiries, arrange inspections, prepare quotations, plan and deliver agreed work, communicate about projects and keep records where necessary. Please avoid sending identity documents, banking details or other sensitive information unless specifically needed and requested through an appropriate channel. This website does not currently provide a customer registration form or online payment form.

2. Sharing, protection, retention and your rights

Project information may be accessed by authorised team members and shared with contractors or service providers where required to handle your enquiry or carry out agreed work, or where disclosure is required by law. WhatsApp and Instagram process messages under their own privacy terms, which may involve processing outside the UAE. We use reasonable organisational and technical safeguards and keep information only for as long as needed for the stated purposes and applicable record-keeping duties. Subject to applicable law, you may ask what personal information we hold about you, request correction or deletion, object to or restrict certain uses, or withdraw consent where consent applies. Send a request via our published WhatsApp contact; we may need to verify that the information concerns you. We may update this policy and publish the revised version on this page.
